# OCEAN Research Gates

This page separates executable repository capabilities from evidence still
required for a scientific performance claim.

## Implemented and regression-tested

- SourcePacket v2, PaperBundle, RunManifest, ValidationCard, adjudication, and
  interoperability schemas;
- safe environment and credential checks;
- variant, target-disease, and manuscript-reliability workflows;
- evidence independence, circularity, leakage, claim-to-validation, and
  evidence-diff detectors;
- statistics, availability, citation-scope, and citation-entailment audits;
- checksum-linked Harbor ledger and Harbor-to-Skill distillation gate;
- formal contract benchmark, permissive control, report comparison,
  repeated-run aggregation, ablation summaries, case-intake validation, and
  guarded leaderboard generation;
- biomedical, biological, clinical, biomedical-AI, omics, drug, materials,
  chemistry, and engineering evidence routing.

These statements describe software behavior covered by repository tests. They
do not describe scientific or model performance.

## Protocol-ready, no result claimed

- two-reviewer blinded human adjudication;
- disagreement resolution and ambiguous-case handling;
- external researcher or laboratory validation;
- repeated multi-model evaluation;
- prospective case studies;
- public leaderboard submission.

The repository contains protocols and validators for these activities but no
fabricated completion records.

## Evidence required before a performance paper

| Gate | Minimum public evidence |
|---|---|
| Task set | At least 100 traceable, permitted, blinded, expert-adjudicated cases |
| Conditions | Base, tools-only, instructions-only, SourcePacket, executable workflow, and human-checkpoint conditions |
| Repetition | Pre-specified repeated runs with failures retained |
| Human review | Two independent blinded experts per case plus adjudication |
| Reliability | Unsupported-claim, citation, circularity, leakage, validation, and reproducibility metrics |
| Efficiency | Token, elapsed-time, and provider-reported cost records |
| Real use | Three distinct prospective research cases with author decisions preserved |
| External validity | At least one independent group following the external validation protocol |
| Statistics | Confidence intervals and inter-rater agreement suitable for the observed labels |

Until these gates are met, OCEAN may be described as an evidence-control
workflow with tested software contracts, not as a scientifically superior
research agent.

## Evidence boundary

Repository CI and the formal 30-case suite establish regression behavior only.
They do not substitute for 100 scientific cases, blinded experts, prospective
research use, or external validation.
